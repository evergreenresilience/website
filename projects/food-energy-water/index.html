Food-Energy-Water Nexus

Understanding the interconnected governance of food, energy, and water systems in Alberta

Water GovernanceFood SystemsPolicy NarrativesDigital AgricultureAlberta
About This Project

Food production, energy generation, and freshwater availability are deeply entangled systems. Decisions in one domain inevitably ripple through others — yet they are typically governed and studied in silos.

What we're doing:

We combine quantitative analysis of agricultural and hydrological datasets with qualitative social science: interviews, surveys, stakeholder consultations, and narrative policy analysis. This mixed-methods approach lets us move from "what is changing" to "why, and what can be done about it."

Example questions we address:

  • How do agricultural, water, and energy policies interact and sometimes come in conflict in Alberta and the broader Canadian Prairies?
  • How can we improve agricultural water sustainability in Alberta and the broader Canadian Prairies, and the role of digitalization?
  • What is the role of narrative and trust in shaping farmer and stakeholder responses to climate-adaptive governance?
  • What are the pathways toward regenerative agriculture that reduce pressure across all three systems simultaneously?
